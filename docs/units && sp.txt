这是我计划的，openra d2k的三个faction

Atreides - 特点，多才多艺，数值平衡
一.Infantry
 - public: engineer, light_infantry, trooper(可对空)
 - unique: grenadier

二.Light Vehicles
  - normal: Sand Runner(前文提及的hunter改名), Missile quad

三.Heavy Vehicles
  - public: Spice Harvester, Atreides Combat Tank, Mobile Construction Vehicle
  - update heavy factor: Siege Tank
  - technology 1: Missile Tank(优先地面有威胁的车辆、其次攻击Ornithopter、然后攻击Harvester、最后carryall、最后frigate)
  - technology 2: Sonic Tank
  
四.Aircraft
  - Carryall, Ornithopter(可以在aircraft内建造，可以控制，使用重力航弹)
 
五.Support power
 - infantry: fremen, officer, worm rider。
 - skill: Airstrike
 
六.Passive bonus
   None
   
七.Building
   unique: Sietch
 

Ordos - 特点，速度取胜，出其不意，高风险、高收益
一.Infantry
 - public: engineer, light_infantry, trooper(可对空)
 - unique: worm_thumper(deploy时进入隐形，一段时间后召唤1只沙虫)

二.Light Vehicles
  - normal: Raider Trike, Missile Quad
  - technology 1: Stealth Raider Trike, crawler(具备基础攻击能力，可以运送saboteur)

三.Heavy Vehicles
  - public: Spice Harvester, Ordos Combat Tank, Mobile Construction Vehicle
  - update heavy factor: Flamer(替代siege tank，对light vehicle更具威胁)
  - technology 1: -
  - technology 2: Deviator(优先地面有威胁的车辆、其次攻击Ornithopter、然后攻击carryall、最后Harvester、最后frigate)

四.Aircraft
  - Carryall

五.Support power
 - infantry: saboteur(high tech factor update之后解锁)。
 - air force: Smugglers Dropship
   一艘frigate：包含1*Devastator、1*Sonic Tank、1*Missile tank、1*Siege tank、1*Flamer。
   一艘carryall：包含3*light_infantry、1*trooper、1*grenadier。
   一艘carryall：包含1*Heavy quad, 2*Raider Trike, 2*Sand Runner。
   经ds研判无法做到仅能投放到有视野的地方，那么pvp时会导致投放到玩家家里，那么需要设置为长CD。需要升级smuggler starport解锁，升级金额是这些部队的总价值。升级时间和CD时间为生产frigate中所有tank的总时间，按两个heavy factor时的生产时间计算。
 - skill: Chaos bomb
 
六.Passive bonus
   Smugglers starport

七.Building
   unique: Smugglers starport
 

Harkonnen - 特点，适合正面战场，重火力碾压
一.Infantry
 - public: engineer, light_infantry, trooper(可对空)
 - unique: sardaukar

二.Light Vehicles
   - normal: Trike
   - technology 1: Heavy quad

三.Heavy Vehicles
  - public: Spice Harvester, Harkonnen Combat Tank, Mobile Construction Vehicle
  - update heavy factor: Siege Tank
  - technology 1: Missile Tank(优先地面有威胁的车辆、其次攻击Ornithopter、然后攻击Harvester、最后carryall、最后frigate)
  - technology 2: Devastator
  
四.Aircraft
  - Carryall

五.Support power
   - skill: Death Hand，Rally sardaukar(需要是非常强大的buf技能，弥补sardaukar打不过light infrantry的不足)

六.Passive bonus
   None
   
七.Building
   unique: palace corrino
